Our story

North Star Bakery was created to bring fresh baking and a welcoming neighbourhood atmosphere together. We believe a loaf of bread, a pastry shared over coffee, or a cake for a celebration can help bring people together.

Our sourcing philosophy

We aim to choose quality ingredients and work with dependable suppliers. We value freshness, careful preparation, and clear information about the products we make. Customers with allergy questions are encouraged to contact us before ordering.

Our team

Our bakery team includes bakers who prepare daily products, decorators who help create celebration cakes, and front-of-house staff who welcome customers and answer questions. We work together to make each visit friendly and helpful.
